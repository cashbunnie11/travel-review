Travel Tips

Quick answers to common questions before you go.

June to September is best for hiking and lakes. December to March is best for skiing and snowy villages. Spring and autumn are quieter and cheaper.

Swiss trains, buses and boats are punctual and well connected. A Swiss Travel Pass gives unlimited travel and free entry to many museums, and is worth it if you plan several long rides.

Yes, it is one of the pricier countries in Europe. Save money with supermarket picnics, tap water (it is safe and free), hostels, and booking mountain trains early for discounts.

Layers, a waterproof jacket, sturdy shoes, sunscreen and sunglasses (the sun is strong at altitude), a power adapter (Type J) and a reusable water bottle.

About This Project

Swiss Escapes is a beginner-friendly, frontend-only website made with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. It helps travellers (students, backpackers, families and first-time visitors) quickly compare popular places in Switzerland.

Place descriptions are written as sample reviews for a learning project, and the pictures are original SVG illustrations. Replace them with your own photos and words to make the site truly yours.

  • ✓ Responsive on phone, tablet and desktop
  • ✓ Image carousel with swipe, dots and autoplay
  • ✓ Filter, search, favourites and review modal
  • ✓ No backend, database or login needed
